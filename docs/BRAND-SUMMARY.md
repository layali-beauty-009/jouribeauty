---

## Identity

**جوري للجمال** is not a generic beauty shop — it is a **serum specialist** with **pharmacy-level clarity**: every bottle names the skin problem, the active ingredient, and how to use it.

We speak like a **trusted Gulf pharmacy counter** — calm, precise, evidence-minded — not like a loud influencer brand.

**One line:** *صيدلية سيرومات لبشرة الخليج — مكوّنات واضحة، روتين بسيط، ثقة قبل الدفع.*

---

## Branding & positioning

| Pillar | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Authority** | Clinical tone, ingredient %, “مشكل ← حل”, no miracle cures |
| **Trust** | COD, order confirmation, 30-day satisfaction, vegan/cruelty-free where true |
| **Gulf-native** | AC, sun, dehydration, tired eyes — problems our ICP lives daily |
| **Ownership** | We *make* these three serums; one brand, three formulas, no marketplace noise |

**Positioning vs market:**  
Between a **pharmacy derm shelf** (trust) and **premium Gulf DTC** (desire). We sell **serums only**, with the credibility of a pharmacist and the warmth of Khaleeji copy.

**ICP:** Women and men 22–45 in UAE/KSA — want results but fear scams; need **proof, Arabic clarity, and pay on delivery**.

**Keep from current site:** problem→solution framing, three focused serums, ingredient transparency, COD, UAE/Gulf climate story.  
**Elevate:** pharmacy visual language, stronger “lab/clinic” trust, unified **جوري للجمال** naming everywhere.
