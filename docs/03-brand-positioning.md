# Brand & Positioning

## Brand identity

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Arabic name | **جوري للجمال** |
| English name | **Jouri Beauty** |
| Domain | namabeauty.shop |
| Category | Premium targeted serums (face + eye) |
| Market | **KSA primary**; Gulf-wide resonance (AC, sun, dust) |

## Positioning statement

**For** Gulf women and men (25–45) whose skin looks tired, dull, or aged because of **AC, sun, and stress**,  
**Jouri** is the **branded serum house** that sells **three focused formulas** (anti-aging, barrier repair, eye)  
**Unlike** random dropship skincare, Jouri **names the problem in your dialect**, shows **ingredients and routine**, and lets you **pay on delivery** with **bundle savings**.

## Brand pillars (use on About + footer + LP)

1. **مصمم لجو الخليج** — AC dehydration, indoor dryness, outdoor sun  
2. **تركيبة واضحة** — hero ingredient on every pack and page  
3. **روتين بسيط** — one serum per concern; bundles for commitment  
4. **ثقة قبل الدفع** — COD, confirmation call, clear totals  
5. **قيمة العبوات** — 2 and 3 packs = real savings (279 / 349)  

## Visual authority

- Feel: **clean clinic-luxury** — not pharmacy cheap, not influencer neon  
- Palette: deep navy/emerald + soft cream + gold accent (see design system)  
- Logo: text-only in header; circle monogram **ن** or stylized **J**  
- Photography direction (when client adds): Gulf skin tones, soft light, product-in-hand UGC style  

## Trust & certification display

Use a **Trust Strip** on home + bottom of LP:

| Badge | Copy (AR) | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| COD | الدفع عند الاستلام | Always visible |
| Delivery | توصيل داخل السعودية | Adjust if UAE later |
| Quality | معايير جودة وتعبئة آمنة | No specific ISO unless provided |
| Vegan | مناسب للنباتيين | If formula supports |
| Cruelty-free | لم يُختبر على الحيوانات | Standard beauty claim |
| Returns | ضمان رضا ٣٠ يوماً | Policy page link |

**Do not** claim "FDA approved" or "علاج" — use **عناية / روتين / يساعد على مظهر**.

## Emotional territory

| Pain (feel) | Promise (feel) |
|-------------|----------------|
| "وجهي باهت وكأني ما نمت" | إشراقة وملمس أنعم |
| "بشرتي مشدودة من المكيف" | رطوبة واسترجاع حاجز |
| "عيوني تعبانة ومنتفخة" | مظهر أهدأ وأفتح |

Copy must **name the pain in one line**, then **offer hope without medical guarantee**.
